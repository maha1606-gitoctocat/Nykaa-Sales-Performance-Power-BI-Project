#  Nykaa Sales Analysis Dashboard

##  Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Sales Analysis Dashboard** for Nykaa, built to track and evaluate business performance across key dimensions such as **revenue, profit, customer segments, and geography**.

##  Project Objectives
-  Analyze overall **revenue, profit, and order volume**
-  Identify **top-performing categories and products**
-  Compare **regional sales performance**
-  Understand **customer segmentation (gender-based)**
-  Track **year-wise growth trends**
-  Highlight **high and low performing cities**
-  Provide insights to improve **business strategy & profitability**

##  Key Metrics
| Metric | Value |
|------|------|
|  Total Revenue | **5M** |
|  Total Orders | **2K** |
|  Profit | **1M** |
|  Current YTD Revenue | **836K** |

##  Dashboard Features
-  Interactive filters (**City, Membership**)
-  KPI cards for quick performance overview
-  Category & region-based revenue breakdown
-  Year-wise revenue & profit trends
-  Gender-based revenue distribution
-  Product-level performance insights
-  Geographic sales distribution (Map)
-  City-wise ranking & comparison

##  Key Insights

###  Revenue & Profit Trends
- Revenue shows a **steady growth trend**, peaking around **2020**
- A **dip post-2020** followed by gradual recovery
- Profit is stable but **not proportional to revenue**, indicating **cost inefficiencies**

###  Category Performance
-  **Skincare** is the top revenue-generating category
-  **Makeup** performs strongly
-  **Haircare** shows lower contribution → growth opportunity

###  Regional Insights
-  **West & North regions** lead in revenue contribution
-  Some regions underperform → **expansion potential**

###  Gender-Based Analysis
-  Female customers: **~53% revenue**
-  Male customers: **~47% revenue**
- Balanced distribution with **female dominance**

###  City-Level Insights
-  Top Cities: **Jaipur, Delhi, Mumbai**
-  Low-performing: **Patna, Bangalore**
-  Strong **urban sales dominance**

###  Product Performance
**Top-Selling Products:**
-  Lakme Lipstick  
-  Maybelline Mascara  
-  Plum Serum  

##  Business Recommendations
-  Expand **Haircare category**
-  Improve **cost efficiency** to boost margins
-  Target **low-performing cities**
-  Leverage **female customer base**
-  Strengthen **top-performing regions**

##  Tools & Technologies
-  Power BI  
-  Excel  
-  Data Modeling & DAX  

##  Conclusion
This dashboard provides a **holistic view of Nykaa’s sales performance**, enabling stakeholders to make **data-driven decisions**, identify growth opportunities, and optimize business strategies.
